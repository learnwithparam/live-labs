# Ratings and standing

## Customer rating

Your customer rating is the average of your last 100 rated deliveries. Ratings from deliveries where the store made the order late are removed automatically.

## Completion rate

Completion rate is the share of your last 100 accepted orders you delivered. Below 80% you lose access to scheduled shifts for two weeks.

## Deactivation

Accounts are deactivated for fraud, for unsafe driving reported and confirmed by support, or for a customer rating below 4.2 over your last 100 deliveries after one warning. You can appeal a deactivation within 10 days.
