# Cancelled orders

## Paid for cancelled orders

If the customer or the store cancels after you have picked up the order, you receive the full base pay and any peak pay. If they cancel before pickup, you receive $2 when you were already at the store.

## Keep or return the food

When an order is cancelled after pickup, the app tells you whether to return it. If it does not ask you to return it, you may keep the food. Never deliver a cancelled order to the customer.
