# Getting paid

## Weekly payout

Earnings from Monday to Sunday are paid to your bank every Wednesday at no cost. A new bank account takes up to 7 days to verify, and the first payout to it can be one week late.

## Instant payout

Instant payout sends your earnings to a debit card once per day for a fee of $1.99. It is available after your first 5 deliveries. Instant payout does not work with a bank account, only a debit card.

## Missing pay

If a delivery is missing from your earnings, wait 24 hours, then report it from the delivery's page in the app under Earnings, Report missing pay. Reports older than 30 days cannot be reviewed.
