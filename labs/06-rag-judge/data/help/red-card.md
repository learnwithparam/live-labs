# The Swiftbite Red Card

## What the Red Card is for

Some stores need you to pay for the order with the Swiftbite Red Card. The card is loaded with the exact order total when you arrive at the store.

## Card declined

If the Red Card is declined, do not pay with your own money. Tap Card declined in the app; support will reload the card or cancel the order, and you are paid as for a cancellation after pickup.

## Lost card

Report a lost Red Card in the app under Account, Red Card. A new card arrives in 5 to 7 days. You can still take orders that do not need it.
