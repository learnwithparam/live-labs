# Staying safe

## Report an incident

If you are in danger, call emergency services first. Then use the Safety button in the app, which shares your location with Swiftbite's safety team, who respond within 5 minutes.

## Accidents

If you have an accident while on a delivery, report it in the app within 24 hours. Swiftbite's accident cover pays medical costs up to $1,000,000 while you are on an active delivery.

## Weather

You may decline or unassign orders during severe weather alerts with no effect on your completion rate.
