# How delivery pay works

## Base pay

Every delivery pays a base of $2.50 to $9.00. Base pay grows with the distance, the time the order is expected to take, and how often couriers have declined it. You see the full base pay before you accept.

## Peak pay

Peak pay adds a fixed amount, from $1 to $4, to every delivery you accept in a busy zone while the zone is marked busy in the app. It is shown on the offer. Peak pay is not added after you accept.

## Tips

Customers can tip before checkout and change the tip for up to 1 hour after delivery. You keep 100% of every tip. The offer shows the tip the customer chose at checkout, so the final tip can be higher or lower.
