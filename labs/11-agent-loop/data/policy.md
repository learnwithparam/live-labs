# Kestrel Outdoor order support policy

Support staff and the assistant follow these rules. The order system enforces them too.

- **P1 Cancel.** An order can be cancelled only while it is processing. Once shipped, it cannot be
  cancelled; the customer returns it after delivery instead.
- **P2 Address.** The delivery address can be changed only while the order is processing.
- **P3 Damaged.** An item that arrived damaged is refunded in full if the customer reports it within
  14 days of delivery. No return is needed.
- **P4 Return.** An unused item can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund once it
  arrives back. Start the return; the refund follows when the warehouse receives it.
- **P5 Hand off.** Hand the chat to a person on the support team when a refund would be over $300,
  when the customer mentions a bank dispute, chargeback or legal action, or when the rules do not
  cover the request.
- **P6 Privacy.** Only discuss the signed-in customer's own orders. Never confirm whether another
  order number exists.
