title: Company bike leasing now pays for four in ten employee cargo bikes
publisher: Dienstrad Report, a leasing industry bulletin
date: 2026-01-29

Company bike leasing financed 41 percent of the cargo bikes that employees bought in Germany in 2025. An employee pays the lease from gross salary, which cuts the cost of a 5,000 euro cargo bike by about a third over three years. The four largest leasing firms list only models that have an EN 17860 test report. To be listed, a brand signs a framework contract with each leasing firm, which takes two to four months.
