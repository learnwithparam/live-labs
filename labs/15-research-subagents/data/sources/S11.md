title: Moving e-bikes across the Dutch-German border
publisher: Freight Notes Benelux, a logistics newsletter
date: 2026-03-27

Shipping one boxed cargo bike from the Netherlands to a German dealer costs between 95 and 140 euros by pallet freight. E-bike batteries are lithium-ion dangerous goods, so carriers require each battery type to pass UN 38.3 testing and to travel with a hazard label. Most carriers deliver within two to three working days to addresses within 600 km of Utrecht. Brands that ship four or more bikes per pallet pay about 60 percent less per bike. A battery shipped on its own as a spare part needs the same paperwork as one fitted to a bike.
