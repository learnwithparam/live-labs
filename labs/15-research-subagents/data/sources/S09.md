title: Berlin reopens its cargo bike grant for households and small firms
publisher: Berliner Stadtbote, a city newspaper
date: 2026-04-03

Berlin reopened its cargo bike grant on 1 April 2026. Private households can receive up to 1,000 euros toward an electric cargo bike, and small businesses up to 2,500 euros. The budget for 2026 is 3 million euros, which the city expects to fund about 2,400 bikes. Only bikes bought from a dealer with a shop in Berlin qualify, and the dealer files the application with the buyer. In 2023 the previous grant ran out of money within eleven days.
