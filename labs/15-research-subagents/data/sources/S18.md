title: One in five young families is thinking about a cargo bike
publisher: Umfragewerk, an online polling firm
date: 2026-05-20

Twenty-two percent of German parents with children under six said they are considering buying a cargo bike in the next two years. Sixty-one percent of them named the price as the main obstacle, and 24 percent named a safe place to park it. Interest was highest in Berlin, Hamburg, Munich, Cologne and Leipzig. The poll asked 3,100 parents online in April 2026.
