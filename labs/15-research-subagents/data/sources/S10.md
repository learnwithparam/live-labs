title: Cargo bike grants after the federal programme
publisher: Bike Policy Notes, a policy newsletter
date: 2024-03-15

The federal grant for commercial cargo bikes, which paid 25 percent of the price up to 2,500 euros, closed to new applications in February 2024. No federal replacement has been announced. Berlin's own cargo bike grant ran out of money in 2023, and the city says it will not return. Munich and Leipzig still run small city grants for businesses, each with a budget of less than 1 million euros a year. Grant rules change often, so sellers should check each city's page before promising a discount.
