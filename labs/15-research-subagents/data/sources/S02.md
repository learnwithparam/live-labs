title: Is the cargo bike boom over? Sales fell 12 percent in our shop tally
publisher: Pedal Ledger, an independent cycling blog
date: 2026-01-22

Our tally of 38 bike shops in Hamburg and Cologne found that cargo bike sales fell 12 percent in 2025. Several owners told us families now buy used cargo bikes instead of new ones. The sample covers only shops that answered our email survey, and it does not include online sellers or the big chains. One Cologne shop said it sold 40 cargo bikes in 2025 against 61 the year before. We will repeat the tally next winter with more cities.
