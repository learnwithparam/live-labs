title: Used cargo bikes: prices and listings in 2025
publisher: Radmarkt Gebraucht, a used bike marketplace's price index
date: 2026-01-12

Listings of used cargo bikes on our marketplace rose to about 9,800 at the end of 2025, up 35 percent on the year. The average asking price of a used electric cargo bike was 2,650 euros, about half the new price. Most used bikes sold were three to five years old. Sellers most often gave a child outgrowing the box as the reason to sell.
