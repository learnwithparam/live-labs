title: Austria's cargo bike market grows on city grants
publisher: Alpenrad Markt, an Austrian trade report
date: 2026-03-02

Austrian cargo bike sales grew 14 percent in 2025 to 19,000 units, helped by grants in Vienna and Graz. Vienna pays up to 800 euros toward a cargo bike for households. Austrian buyers favour three-wheel models more than German buyers do, at 44 percent of sales. Most brands serve Austria from a German warehouse.
