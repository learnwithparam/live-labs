title: Paris finishes its 2026 bike lane plan
publisher: Ville Cyclable
date: 2026-06-05

Paris completed 180 km of new protected bike lanes under its 2021 to 2026 plan. Bike trips in the city grew 11 percent in 2025. Cargo bikes now make up about 4 percent of the bikes counted on the busiest lanes. The city will widen lanes on its main boulevards in 2027.
