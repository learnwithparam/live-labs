title: E-mountain bike motors compared for 2026
publisher: Trail Technik
date: 2026-04-25

We tested six e-mountain bike motors for torque, noise and battery range. The strongest motor gave 95 Nm of torque, the quietest was the least powerful. Range on our test loop ran from 48 to 71 km on a 750 Wh battery. Lighter motors suit riders who climb less.
