title: Repairing carbon frames: when it is safe
publisher: Werkstatt Wissen
date: 2025-08-21

A cracked carbon frame can often be repaired for 300 to 600 euros, far less than a new frame. Repair shops scan the frame with ultrasound before they quote. Damage near the head tube or the bottom bracket is the hardest to repair safely. A repaired frame usually weighs 20 to 40 grams more.
