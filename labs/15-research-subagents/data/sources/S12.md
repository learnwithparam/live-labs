title: Cargo bike theft and what cover costs in German cities
publisher: Versicherung Mobil, an insurance trade journal
date: 2026-02-20

Insurers paid theft claims on about 1 in 60 insured cargo bikes in German cities in 2025. Theft cover for a 5,000 euro cargo bike costs between 120 and 190 euros a year. Insurers give a discount of up to 15 percent when the bike carries a GPS tracker. Hamburg and Berlin had the highest claim rates, and smaller cities about half as many. Several insurers now ask for a frame number registered with the brand before they write a policy.
