title: What German bike dealers want from a new cargo brand
publisher: Velohandel Today, a trade magazine for bike retailers
date: 2026-02-09

German independent bike dealers expect a margin of 30 to 35 percent on cargo bikes. Most dealers want the brand to hold spare parts in Germany and to ship them within 48 hours. Dealers said they drop brands whose spare parts take more than a week to arrive, because a family without its cargo bike cannot take children to daycare. A new brand is also expected to train workshop staff before the first bike is sold. Dealers rated warranty handling as the second biggest reason to keep or drop a brand, after spare parts.
