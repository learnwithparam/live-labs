title: EN 17860 and the road rules: selling cargo bikes in Germany
publisher: Normen Praxis, a newsletter on product standards
date: 2025-12-02

EN 17860 is the European safety standard for cargo bikes, with parts covering frame strength, brakes and stability when loaded. German dealers and leasing firms increasingly ask for a test report against EN 17860 before they list a model. A cargo bike whose motor assists only up to 25 km/h with at most 250 watts counts as a bicycle under German road law, so it needs no registration, number plate or rider licence. Cargo bikes faster than that are classed as mopeds and need insurance and a plate. Test labs in Germany quote six to ten weeks and 8,000 to 15,000 euros for a full EN 17860 report on one model.
