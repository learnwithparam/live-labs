title: Two-wheel cargo bikes in Germany: the brands and the prices in 2026
publisher: Cargo Cycle Review, a buyer's guide magazine
date: 2026-05-04

Three brands lead the German two-wheel electric cargo segment: Fjordline Cargo, Haldor Bikes and Mulde Raeder. Fjordline's Long 2 sells for 5,290 euros, Haldor's Load 60 for 6,990 euros and Mulde's Kiste for 4,490 euros. Together they held about 46 percent of two-wheel electric cargo bike sales in 2025. Mulde Raeder builds its bikes in Saxony and promises delivery to the dealer within two weeks. Below 4,000 euros the field is crowded with direct-to-consumer brands that sell online and offer no dealer service. Most mid-priced models now come with a 545 Wh battery and a mid-drive motor.
