title: Dutch cargo bike sales slip as big cities fill up
publisher: Fietsbranche Nieuws, a Dutch trade paper
date: 2026-02-14

Dutch cargo bike sales fell 4 percent in 2025 to 61,000 units, as most families in Amsterdam, Utrecht and Rotterdam who want one already have one. Kitebird Cargo sold 3,900 bikes in the Netherlands and Belgium in 2025, up from 3,400 the year before. Dutch dealers expect replacement sales to carry the market from 2027, as the first electric cargo bikes bought in 2018 wear out. Prices in the Netherlands run about 6 percent below German prices for the same models.
