title: Haldor Bikes recalls 3,100 cargo bikes over cracked frame welds
publisher: Rheinland Business Daily
date: 2026-06-17

Haldor Bikes is recalling 3,100 Load 60 cargo bikes sold in Germany and Austria after reports of cracks near the frame's front weld. The company said no rider had been seriously hurt. Haldor expects the recall to cost about 4 million euros, including replacement frames and dealer labour. Dealers say customers now ask whether a cargo bike has been tested to EN 17860 before they buy. Haldor's sales in the first half of 2026 fell by about a fifth, according to two dealers in Munich.
