title: Shared e-scooters in Madrid: rides fall after the parking rules
publisher: Movilidad Urbana Hoy
date: 2026-04-08

Rides on shared e-scooters in Madrid fell 18 percent in the year after the city limited parking to marked bays. Two of the four operators left the city. Riders took more trips on shared e-bikes instead, which rose 9 percent. The city plans 400 new parking bays by the end of 2026.
