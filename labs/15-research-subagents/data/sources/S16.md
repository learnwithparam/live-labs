title: Couriers add cargo bikes for city deliveries
publisher: Last Mile Logistics Weekly
date: 2026-07-15

Parcel firm Rapido Kurier plans to add 600 electric cargo bikes in Hamburg, Munich and Leipzig by the end of 2027. Fleet buyers want a service contract that gets a broken bike repaired or replaced within four hours. Most courier fleets lease their bikes for three years rather than buy them. Rapido said two-wheel models suit narrow old-town streets better than three-wheel ones.
