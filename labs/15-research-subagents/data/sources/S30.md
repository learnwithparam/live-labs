title: Kitebird kite school opens a second beach on Sylt
publisher: Nordsee Sport
date: 2026-06-22

Kitebird, the kitesurfing school on Sylt, opened a second teaching beach at List in June 2026. A three-day beginner course costs 390 euros. The school trains about 1,200 students a year. It also rents boards and wetsuits by the day.
