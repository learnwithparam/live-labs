title: US road bike sales cool after the pandemic surge
publisher: American Bike Retail Quarterly
date: 2026-02-26

Road bike sales in the United States fell 7 percent in 2025, the third fall in a row since the pandemic peak. Gravel bikes were the only drop-bar category to grow. Shops cut prices by an average of 14 percent to clear 2024 stock. Analysts expect the market to steady in 2026.
