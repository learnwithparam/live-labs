title: Dealer survey 2025: cargo bikes on the shop floor
publisher: Verbund deutscher Zweiradhaendler, a dealer cooperative
date: 2025-09-30

Of 420 member dealers surveyed, 64 percent stock at least one cargo bike brand, up from 49 percent in 2022. Thirty-nine percent plan to add a new cargo brand in 2027, most often to replace a brand with slow spare parts. Dealers asked that a brand place at least two demo bikes in each store, because most customers will not buy without a test ride. The median dealer sold 34 cargo bikes in 2024. Dealers in the north reported longer test-ride waiting lists than dealers in the south.
