title: German cargo bike sales grew 8 percent in 2025
publisher: Fahrradwirtschaft Monitor, the trade association's annual market report
date: 2026-03-10

Dealers and direct sellers in Germany sold 231,000 cargo bikes in 2025, up 8 percent from 214,000 in 2024. Electric models made up 91 percent of those sales. Two-wheel long-john designs were the largest group at 54 percent of units, ahead of three-wheel box bikes at 31 percent and longtails at 15 percent. The average selling price of an electric cargo bike was 5,420 euros, up 3 percent on the year. The association counts sales reported by 1,140 member dealers and the six largest direct sellers, which together cover an estimated 88 percent of the market. It expects growth of 5 to 7 percent in 2026 and 2027, slower than the double-digit years before 2024.
