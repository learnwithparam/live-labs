title: Who buys cargo bikes in Germany: the 2025 buyer survey
publisher: Mobilitaetspanel Insights, a consumer research panel
date: 2025-11-18

Families with children under six bought 58 percent of new cargo bikes in 2025. Businesses, including couriers, tradespeople and care services, bought 27 percent, and the rest went to households without young children. Buyers named a test ride at a local shop as the most important step before buying, at 71 percent. Only 18 percent said they would buy a cargo bike online without a test ride. The median household that bought one lives in a city of more than 500,000 people and owns at most one car. The survey asked 2,050 recent buyers between August and October 2025.
