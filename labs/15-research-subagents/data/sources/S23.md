title: Cycling holidays along the Mosel set a record
publisher: Weinland Tourismus
date: 2025-10-10

Cycle tourists spent 2.1 million nights along the Mosel valley in 2025, a record. Most rented e-bikes for three to five days. Hotels along the river added secure bike rooms and charging points. The region plans a new 40 km riverside path for 2027.
