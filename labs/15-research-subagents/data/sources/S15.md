title: Eleven new cargo brands at the Frankfurt trade fair
publisher: Velohandel Today, a trade magazine for bike retailers
date: 2026-07-01

At the Frankfurt cycle trade fair in June 2026, eleven cargo bike brands showed models for the German market for the first time, the most ever. Most of the newcomers price their two-wheel models between 4,200 and 5,200 euros. Dealers at the fair said they will take on one or two new brands at most, and only those with spare parts in Germany. Several newcomers offered dealers a 36 percent margin to win shelf space.
