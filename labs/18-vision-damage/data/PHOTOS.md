# Photos

Every photo is a real one from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org), taken in the UK, Canada,
India, Switzerland, the United States, France and Germany. Each was resized to at most 640 pixels on its
long side and saved as JPEG; nothing else was changed. The claims, policyholders, accounts and bills in
`claims.json` and `adjuster.json` are written for this lab and belong to no real person.
