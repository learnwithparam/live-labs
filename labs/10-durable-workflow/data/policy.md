# Fernhill Home refund policy

R1. Damaged on arrival. If an item arrives damaged and the customer reports it within 7 days of
delivery, refund the item price and the delivery fee in full. The customer does not return the item.

R2. Change of mind. Within 30 days of delivery, an unused item in its original packaging can be
returned. Refund the item price minus the $49 return pickup fee. The delivery fee is not refunded.

R3. Made to order. Items marked made to order (custom fabric or size) cannot be returned for a change
of mind. Damage on arrival is still covered by R1.

R4. Late delivery. If an order arrived more than 14 days after the promised date, refund the delivery
fee. This can be added to a refund under R1 or R2.

R5. After 30 days. No refund. Offer a repair under the 2-year warranty instead.

R6. Approval. Any refund over $500 needs a store manager's approval before it is paid.
